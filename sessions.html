Plain-word questions

What does the deposit cover?

The 25% deposit holds your date and start time. It comes off your total — the balance shown in your booking summary is what you pay on session day.

What if the weather turns?

Outdoor sessions reschedule once at no charge for rain or storms. Golden-hour light is the point — we would rather move the day than fake the light.

When do we get our photos?

Seven days for the full edited gallery, or 48 hours with the Rush add-on. You get a private online gallery and full-resolution downloads.

Is this a real studio?

No — Golden Hour Studio is a fictional business created for a front-end design portfolio. The booking flow genuinely works in your browser, but no real booking is placed and no payment is taken.
